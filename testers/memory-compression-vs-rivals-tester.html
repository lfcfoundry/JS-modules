What is measured

Six real columns of numbers (64-bit): SEC filing numbers (413,875 values), SBA loan numbers (147,846), COVID confirmed counts and deaths by place and day (161,568 each) and retail quantities and unit prices (95,607 each). The columns were cut from public data files on a cloud machine and are used for testing only. Races: pack the whole column to bytes; unpack the bytes back to the column (checked bit for bit before every race); pack plus unpack from the two medians; and size. Ours is balanced mode (the default for numbers); the size rows also show small mode. Rivals: the browser's own gzip (CompressionStream and DecompressionStream, default level) on the raw bytes, and Pcodec through pcodec-js at level 8. Both sides do the same job (bytes in, bytes out), take turns with the order swapped every round, small jobs are repeated within one timing, and every race has a time budget with at least 9 rounds. The first race gets a longer warm-up. Quick run races fewer rounds per race.
