What is measured

All five nested views (icicle, treemap, sunburst, circle pack, tree) on five made-up trees (mixed, two children per item, a few huge folders, long path-like names, rows in shuffled order) at 1,000, 10,000 and 100,000 items. Both libraries do the same job from the same rows and take turns. Each builds its own items from the rows inside the timed job: d3 with its stratify, netflow-hierarchy with a plain loop that makes each item with its parent's name and its seven output fields already present, as its README advises (no list of links on either side). Children are sorted biggest first on both sides, and icicle is also timed with no sorting on either side. The Quick run races only the 23 closest of the 90 races, with more rounds each. netflow-hierarchy is loaded from this site and d3-hierarchy from jsDelivr.
