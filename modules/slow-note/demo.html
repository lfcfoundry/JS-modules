slow-note demo

The note keeps the last few slow or broken moments of this page, on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere. To keep the demo quick it starts noting 1.5 seconds after load (a real page waits 4 seconds so the busy start-up is left out). The same kind of moment is noted at most once a second.
