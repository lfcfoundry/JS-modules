Four tiny helpers. Everything here runs offline from the four files next to this page. Sections 2 and 4 have a one-button automatic check that tells you in plain words what happened.

1. EvenDt: raw vs smoothed frame gaps

The ball moves by the smoothed gap and never stops, even while the texture below is being built.

2. QualityGovernor: level follows real frame times

average gap 0 ms level 0
Drag the load up: after a short delay the level steps down; set it back to 0 and it climbs again. This demo uses shorter delays than the defaults so you can see it.
